## Rules for staying out of trouble

1. **No real logos, brands or named people in artwork.** The archived design
   briefs in `SHIRT_DESIGNS_CORRECTED.md` call for "corporate logos" and
   "financial institution logos" — that is trademark infringement and gets
   listings pulled. The current seven designs are clean. Keep them that way.
2. **Satire reads as satire.** The line works because it mocks surveillance
   culture from inside the voice of the regime. Copy that drops the character
   and starts making factual claims about real events changes the category
   from apparel to political content, with the ad-policy problems that brings.
3. **Don't claim scarcity you can't honour.** "Batch limited" is brand voice,
   not a countdown timer. Print-on-demand never actually runs out, and a fake
   timer is a marketplace policy violation on most platforms.
